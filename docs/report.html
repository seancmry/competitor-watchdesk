What changed overnight?

We watched three pages with saved sample content. Nothing here calls the live web. First we saved a baseline. A second look found no change. Then we simulated a careers update so the desk could show a real “changed” row.

In this run, 1 page changed and 2 stayed the same. The desk writes a short report you can open, share, or feed to a weekday morning check.

What this is worth

  • You stop opening the same careers and changelog tabs every Monday hoping to notice a difference.
  • A change is named in plain language — for example a new role heading — not a wall of HTML.
  • Quiet pages stay quiet. Only the ones that moved need your attention.
  • The same report can later wake Slack, a sheet, or a spoken brief. The judgment stays here.
